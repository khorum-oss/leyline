# Open questions

**All eight are closed.** Each was resolved during the stage that needed it, and
each has a decision record. The table stays as the trail: what was open, what was
chosen, and where the reasoning lives.

New questions get added here as they surface, and closed the same way — struck
through, with a pointer to the record that settled them.

Terms appearing in these questions are defined in
[`glossary.md`](glossary.md) — several of the questions turn on a distinction it
draws, such as [policy versus guard](glossary.md#policy).

| ID      | Question                                                                                                                                    | What was decided                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Record                                               | Stage |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| ~~OQ1~~ | ~~**Persistence of applied changes.**~~                                                                                                     | The core persists nothing. The application stores apply and revert records from a trace sink and hands them back; `hydrate()` replays them through policy, so a change no longer permitted is dropped rather than restored. | [0028](decisions/0028-persistence.md)                | 2     |
| ~~OQ2~~ | ~~**Granularity of policy.**~~                                                                                                              | One predicate, `(proposal) => allow \| deny \| confirm`, plus shipped combinators: `allowKinds`, `denyKinds`, `forInitiator`, `requireConfirmation`, `allOf`, `anyOf`, `permissive`, `denyAll`.                             | [0024](decisions/0024-policy-shape.md)               | 2     |
| ~~OQ3~~ | ~~**Renderer identity across bundles.**~~ How a renderer advertises itself for discovery                                                    | A catalogue. Registration metadata — name, description, the surface types or region kinds it will claim — is what makes a renderer addressable at all, which is what gives I3 something to gate against.                    | [0023](decisions/0023-renderer-catalogue.md)         | 3     |
| ~~OQ4~~ | ~~**Confirmation flow.**~~ Who confirms when policy returns "require confirmation", and how                                                 | The proposal stays pending and the decision is the application's. The agent surface reports the pending state rather than resolving it, so nothing confirms itself.                                                         | [0027](decisions/0027-confirmation-flow.md)          | 4     |
| ~~OQ5~~ | ~~**Minimum surface set for v1.**~~                                                                                                         | `form`, `datatable`, `metric-panel`, `link`, `text` — nothing beyond what brief §2 needs.                                                                                                                                   | [0016](decisions/0016-minimum-surface-set.md)        | 1     |
| ~~OQ6~~ | ~~**Trace retention and export.**~~                                                                                                         | A mode-sized ring buffer, `export()` reporting how many events were dropped, sinks attachable at construction so nothing emitted during startup is missed.                                                                  | [0021](decisions/0021-trace-retention-and-export.md) | 2     |
| ~~OQ7~~ | ~~**Initiator identity and trust.**~~ Whether Leyline carries an authenticated identity from the transport, or treats the label as advisory | The label is advisory and says so. Attestation is a separate, non-forgeable fact about the connection, supplied by the host at construction; the policy hook is where anything stronger gets enforced.                      | [0031](decisions/0031-initiator-trust.md)            | 4     |
| ~~OQ8~~ | ~~**Guard evaluation event volume.**~~                                                                                                      | Every kind on by default, `setEnabledKinds` as the lever, `isEnabled` guarding hot paths so a silenced kind costs one boolean.                                                                                              | [0022](decisions/0022-guard-event-volume.md)         | 2     |
